A mobile AI gifting experience that helps users move from uncertain intent to personalized gift matches, comparison, and saving.

01 — Problem

The gift market is full of products — but users still struggle to make the right choice.

02 — Research

Users are not casually browsing gifts — they are trying to find a suitable option under constraints.

🎯
Who am I buying for?
Users need context about their relationship with the recipient.
🎁
What is the gift for?
Occasion shapes what feels appropriate and meaningful.
💰
What price range works?
Budget is a constraint users need to consider from the very beginning.

02 — User Insights

Gift decisions get stuck in three kinds of uncertainty.

Based on interviews with young gift-givers and 17 user feedback tags. Three recurring blockers emerged.

Uncertain
Not sure what the recipient actually likes — hard to tell their real interests and preferences.
Uncertain
Not sure how to compare multiple gift options — users don't know how to evaluate candidates side by side.
Uncertain
Not sure when to prepare — users often procrastinate, missing the right window to buy.

DESIGN QUESTION

How might we help users find the right gift with limited information?

03 — Design Strategies

We translated three uncertainties into three design strategies.

User Pain Point Design Strategy What it enables
Not sure what the recipient likes Guided Filtering Narrows the decision space through relationship, occasion, and budget signals.
Not sure how to compare options AI Comparison Helps users compare candidate gifts more clearly, side by side.
Not sure when to prepare Countdown Reminder Reminds users to prepare in time before the occasion.

FEATURE 01 / AI-GUIDED FILTERING

AI-Guided Filtering

By asking for relationship, occasion, budget, and preference signals, the system narrows the search space before showing recommendations — including product options early in the journey.

FEATURE 02 / AI MATCHING & COMPARISON

AI-Matching & Comparison

Recommendations are presented as ranked matches rather than generic product listings, helping users evaluate options with context about why each gift was suggested.

FEATURE 03 / REMINDER & TIMING

Reminder & Timing

Gift decisions are time-sensitive. Users often know the occasion date before they know what to buy. The reminder feature brings gift ideas back to the home page, helping users prepare in time without adding extra work.
